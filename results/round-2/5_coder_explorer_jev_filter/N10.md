- Develop and hand over a Software Configuration Management Plan (SCMP) — a living document covering: introduction (purpose, scope, terms), SCM management (organisation, responsibilities, authorities, applicable policies and procedures), SCM activities, SCM schedules, SCM resources, and provisions for SCMP maintenance. (source: root/Software Maintenance and Configuration Management/Software Configuration Management Planning and Implementation/Software Configuration Management Plan Development.md) [JEV 0.96]
- Develop a maintenance plan — beginning at the decision to build the system — covering: scope of software maintenance, adaptation of maintenance processes and tools, and identification of the maintenance organisation. - Document and hand over all [SWEBOK excerpt 03ad80de9ff11706d6a7] software/hardware licences and contracts, because these contractual agreements [SWEBOK excerpt a85f62e46bec9af8770c] that the client's team will need to manage. (source: root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md) [JEV 0.91]
- Plan for ongoing documentation as a supporting maintenance activity: the maintainers should continue to produce documentation, perform SCM, verification and validation, problem resolution, SQA, reviews, vulnerability assessments, and audits throughout the maintenance phase. (source: root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Retain documentation for the full maintenance lifetime of the software: the maintenance phase [SWEBOK excerpt e5aabbce3fff8efbd15d], so documentation needed to understand, modify, or support the system should be kept at least that long. (source: root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md) [JEV 0.71]
- Include release/version planning documentation — covering availability dates of individual requests, agreed content of subsequent releases, identified conflicts and alternatives, risk assessments, and back-out plans — to enable the client's team to manage future evolution safely. (source: root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md) [JEV 0.97]
