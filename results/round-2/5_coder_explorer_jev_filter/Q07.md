# Guidelines
- Use mutation testing to evaluate test suite effectiveness: introduce small syntactic changes (mutants) into the system under test and measure how many your test suite detects (kills). The mutation score — the ratio of killed mutants to total generated mutants — directly quantifies your suite's fault-detection power. The underlying 'coupling effect' assumption is that tests capable of finding simple syntactic faults will also uncover more complex real faults. (source: root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Fault Models and Mutation Testing Techniques.md; root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Mutation Testing and Fault Injection Evaluation.md; root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Mutation-Based and Usage-Based Testing Techniques/Mutation Testing as a Testing Criterion.md) [JEV 0.96]
- Use fault injection to estimate test suite effectiveness: artificially introduce known faults into the SUT before testing, then run your test suite and count how many injected faults are revealed. The higher the proportion of injected faults discovered, the stronger the suite's ability to detect real faults. Apply this technique with care, since there is a risk of leaving artificially inserted faults in the system. (source: root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Mutation Testing and Fault Injection Evaluation.md; root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Reliability Growth Models and Test Evaluation.md) [JEV 0.93]

(source: root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Test Effectiveness and Process Levels/Test Suite Effectiveness and Testing Process Integration.md) [JEV 0.94]

- Apply usage-based (operational profile) testing to validate that test cases reflect the real-world usage distribution, so that the test suite evaluates reliability under actual operating conditions. Use statistical sampling to simulate many real usage scenarios, and assess reliability by inferring from [SWEBOK excerpt cdb77e88d493b1903a71] in deployment. (source: root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Mutation-Based and Usage-Based Testing Techniques/Usage-Based Testing Techniques and Statistical Sampling.md; root/Testing Methodologies and Quality Assurance Practices/Software Testing Techniques, Measurement, and Management/Operational Profile Testing for Software Reliability.md) [JEV 0.95]
