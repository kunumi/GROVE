### Question

After each change to a system, how should the team decide what to test again, and at which point of the process?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

Here are guidelines from the SWEBOK Guide on deciding what to retest and when after a change:

---

1. **Perform regression testing to verify that changes have not introduced new faults into previously working software.** [SWEBOK excerpt a91827c9cbf753285363] system after modifications, to ensure that defects have not been introduced or uncovered in unchanged parts. (source: "retesting of a system or component [SWEBOK excerpt 7212333249a6119f83c0]")

2. **Use risk analysis to prioritize which parts of the system to retest.** Not all previously passing tests need to be re-executed; teams should identify areas of the system most likely to be affected by the change and focus testing effort there. (source: "risk-based testing uses risk to prioritize and focus the test effort")

3. **Apply regression test selection techniques to choose a subset of the existing test suite.** Rather than re-running the entire suite, techniques such as traceability from changed code to test cases can be used to select the most relevant tests. (source: "regression test selection techniques choose a subset of existing test cases to rerun")

4. **Retest at every level of the test hierarchy affected by the change** — unit, integration, and system — since a change at a low level may have ripple effects at higher levels. (source: "unit testing, integration testing, system testing, and acceptance testing")

5. **Incorporate regression testing into continuous integration processes** so that retesting occurs automatically and immediately after every change is committed, catching faults as early as possible. (source: "test activities can and should occur throughout the entire software development lifecycle")

6. **Maintain and update the regression test suite as the system evolves,** retiring obsolete tests and adding new ones that cover the changed or added functionality to keep the suite relevant. (source: "the test suite must be maintained and updated as the software evolves")
