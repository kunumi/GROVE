# Guidelines for Choosing Among Three Candidate Libraries

## 1. Recognize this as a multiple-attribute decision problem

When money is not the only criterion, explicitly acknowledge that you have a multiple-attribute decision. Financial cost matters, but other criteria — maturity, community activity, team familiarity — must be formally included rather than handled informally. Trying to collapse everything into cost alone will produce a suboptimal choice.

## 7. Consider non-compensatory techniques when certain criteria are non-negotiable
