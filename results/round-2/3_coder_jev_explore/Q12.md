### Answer

# Summary

Good module organization requires applying principles like separation of concerns, encapsulation, high cohesion, and low coupling to keep software understandable and maintainable. Poorly structured programs can be improved through refactoring—reorganizing internals without changing external behavior—and reverse engineering to recover design understanding.
