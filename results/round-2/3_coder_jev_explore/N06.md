### Question

At the end of each release our bug tracker tells us only how many bugs were found, and that count gives the team nothing to act on. What should we do with our bug records so that they help us introduce fewer bugs in later releases?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary

Bug counts alone are insufficient to drive improvement. Teams should classify defects by type, perform root cause analysis, build defect profiles, apply statistical and trend analysis, record findings, and implement corrective/preventive actions so that measurement data actively guides future process and product quality improvement.

# Guidelines

- Classify bugs by type and category, not just count them; numbers alone without classification are insufficient to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence.
