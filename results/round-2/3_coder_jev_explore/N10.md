### Answer

# Summary

When handing over a finished system, software engineers must prepare documentation for the client (including functional specs, design, test suites, and operating environment details), for end users (safe/unsafe use, warnings, sensitive-data protection), and for their own organization (requirements, design, tools, test specs, problems encountered). Documents must be retained for at least the product's full lifecycle or as long as regulations require.
