### Answer

# Summary

Software in use undergoes several distinct types of maintenance: corrective (fixing faults), preventive (averting future problems), adaptive (adjusting to environmental changes), perfective (improving quality or documentation), additive (adding significant new features), and emergency (temporary fixes pending corrective action). Each type differs in its trigger, scope, and intent.
