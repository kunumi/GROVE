# Summary

After each system change, teams should determine what to retest by analyzing the impact of changes, selecting and prioritizing relevant test cases, and conducting regression testing at appropriate levels. This process applies across all development methodologies, from traditional to Agile/DevOps, and may be supported by traceability, automation, and continuous integration practices.
