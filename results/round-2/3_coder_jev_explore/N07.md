### Question

We are planning a project that depends on a third-party API nobody on the team has used and on a performance target we may not be able to reach. How should we handle these things that could go wrong, both when planning the work and while it is under way?

### Answer

# Summary

When facing unknown third-party APIs and uncertain performance targets, teams should identify and register risks early, analyze probability and impact, build prototypes to explore unknowns, monitor risks continuously throughout the project, and be prepared to adapt plans, apply corrective actions, or recalculate risk exposure as new information emerges.
