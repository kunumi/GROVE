### Question

I am handing over a finished system to a client whose in-house team will keep developing it after delivery. Besides the program itself, what written material should I prepare for the client, for its end users and for my own organization, and for how long should it be retained?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
When handing over a finished system whose client will continue developing it, SWEBOK v4 requires preparing several categories of written material: (1) a **software maintenance plan** (developed during delivery) covering scope, process adaptation, and modification/problem-reporting procedures; (2) a **Software Configuration Management Plan (SCMP)** — a living document maintained across the entire lifecycle; (3) **design work products and rationale** for every non-trivial decision to aid future maintainability; and (4) transition and program-understanding records for the delivering organization. All maintenance-phase documentation must be retained until the software is retired, because the maintenance phase itself "[SWEBOK excerpt ff4f53d58be640e9a31c]."

# Guidelines

- **Prepare a software maintenance plan during development** that specifies [SWEBOK excerpt 2a01d0619f3b482a5266], and addresses scope, process adaptation, and identification of the maintenance organization.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance and Configuration Management/…/Software Maintenance Planning and Cost Estimation.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt dcd77385d2336dc761c2]."*)

- **Develop a concept document first**, to be followed by the full maintenance plan.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt 8451647d6ef5b3aa46ed]…"*)

- **Hand over SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, and hardware/software licence contracts** to the client.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt 7413dc50725b83b67e5b]."*)

- **Produce and hand over an SCMP** covering introduction, SCM management, SCM activities, SCM schedules, SCM resources, and SCMP maintenance provisions; it is a *living document* maintained throughout the lifecycle.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance and Configuration Management/…/Software Configuration Management Plan Development.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt 40e912d8a54f87efe8db]."*)

- **Hand over SCM procedures** that provide verification, validation, and audit of every step needed [SWEBOK excerpt ec4060142fd25283be79].
  (source: `root/…/Configuration Management in Software Maintenance.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt b38776826c48004dbdde]."*)

- **Document and hand over design work products** capturing the domain-problem aspects, solution vocabulary, major decisions taken, and rationale for every non-trivial decision.
  (source: `root/Software Design Concerns, Documentation, and Modeling/…/Recording Software Designs.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt bb1be5a019d0345473f3] … ([SWEBOK excerpt 59eaedf69004d1128ce1]."*)

- **Record rationale for all significant design decisions** to enhance the product's long-term maintainability when the client team makes modifications or enhancements.
  (source: `root/Software Design Concerns, Documentation, and Modeling/…/Recording Software Designs.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt f6d616ee75a3fe549317]."*)

- **Give end users written procedures** for requesting modifications and reporting problems (derived from the maintenance plan).
  (source: `root/…/Software Maintenance Planning and Cost Estimation.md` — same quote as above)

- **Document the maintenance help-desk function** so users and staff can coordinate assessment, prioritization, and costing of modification requests and incidents.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Costs, Metrics, and Quality/Software Maintenance Processes and Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt 734a6d92c2b832ad3bbd]."*)

- **Keep transition records** documenting the controlled, coordinated sequence of activities by which the software was transferred from developer to operations/maintenance team.
  (source: `root/…/Software Maintenance Processes and Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt 2b4dcb17509c796017d9]."*)

- **Retain program-understanding documentation** — what the software does and how its parts work together — to support future impact analysis and change management.
  (source: `root/…/Software Maintenance Processes and Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt ac40b77ff932cfd98fb8]."*)

- **Retain records of all ongoing support activities**: SCM, V&V, problem resolution, SQA, reviews, vulnerability assessments, and audits.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt addeaef604bd87ceebf9]."*)

- **Retain release/version planning records** (dates, agreed content, conflicts, alternatives, risk assessments, back-out plans) for every release throughout maintenance.
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md` — *"[SWEBOK excerpt abb7660974adba649885]."*)

- **Retain all maintenance documentation until the software is retired**: [SWEBOK excerpt 4c4dd3ab42182deade1f]."
  (source: `root/Software Maintenance Planning and Supporting Activities.md`)
