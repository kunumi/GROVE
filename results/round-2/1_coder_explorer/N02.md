### Answer

# Summary
Before committing to a network-service split, the team should run a structured architecture evaluation—using methods such as ATAM—that explicitly models competing quality attributes (performance, cost, modifiability) in a utility tree and traces their trade-offs. Formal analysis, performance simulation, and feasibility prototypes can predict run-time behaviour without writing production code, while architecture reviews and metrics quantify structural risks. All findings, alternatives rejected, and trade-offs accepted should be documented so future maintainers understand the constraints.
