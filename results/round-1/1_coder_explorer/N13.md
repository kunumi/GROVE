### Question

The same kind of failure keeps coming back in production even though every occurrence was patched as soon as it was reported. What methods can the team use to trace the failure back to what is really producing it, so that the fix keeps it from happening again?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
When a failure keeps recurring despite patches, the team is fixing symptoms rather than root causes. SWEBOK v4 recommends combining defect classification, root cause analysis (RCA), statistical quality techniques (Pareto charts, control charts, trend analysis), post-mortems, and iterative process improvement frameworks (PDCA, Six Sigma, Lean, Kaizen) to surface the underlying cause. Product telemetry across all system layers and thorough impact analysis before each fix help ensure that the actual source is identified and that solutions are complete rather than partial.

# Guidelines

- **Establish a defect classification taxonomy.** Numbers of occurrences alone, without classification, are insufficient to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence.
