### Question

After each change to a system, how should the team decide what to test again, and at which point of the process?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
After every change, the team should re-run a **regression test suite** — a selective subset of previously passing tests — to verify that the modification caused no unintended effects and that the system still meets its requirements. Test-case **selection, minimisation, and prioritisation** help control the cost. Timing depends on the development model: in sequential processes, regression testing normally follows integration testing and precedes deployment; in shift-left, Agile, and DevOps contexts it runs **continuously on every build**, preceded by a smoke test to confirm the build itself is healthy.

# Guidelines

- **Regression testing is the fundamental mechanism**: use it to selectively retest after every change to check for unintended effects and compliance with requirements. (source: `root/Software Testing Levels, Objectives, and Approaches/Alpha, Beta, and Regression Testing Procedures.md` — "[SWEBOK excerpt 581a14ed05133bd3b504]")

- **Apply regression at every test level** (unit, integration, system, acceptance), not only at the end: "[SWEBOK excerpt 540c394b1762c9e4a4d8]." (source: `root/Software Testing Levels, Objectives, and Approaches/Alpha, Beta, and Regression Testing Procedures.md`)

- **Standard sequencing**: run regression [SWEBOK excerpt 0f6027292d0f7e6a203b]: "[SWEBOK excerpt 302fdce151c0bdc54f71]." (source: `root/Software Testing Levels, Objectives, and Approaches/Regression Testing in Software Development.md`)

- **Cover non-functional properties too**: include [SWEBOK excerpt 6081dd06be3ad080f185] in the regression suite. (source: `root/Software Testing Levels, Objectives, and Approaches/Regression Testing in Software Development.md`)

- **Select, minimise, and prioritise** to manage cost: because running every test after every change is "quite time-consuming," teams should use test-case selection and minimisation, combined with prioritisation. (source: `root/Software Testing Levels, Objectives, and Approaches/Alpha, Beta, and Regression Testing Procedures.md` and `Regression Testing in Software Development.md`)

- **In shift-left / Agile / DevOps, test as early as possible**: the shift-left movement promotes "[SWEBOK excerpt a893583528935d04e4d4]."
