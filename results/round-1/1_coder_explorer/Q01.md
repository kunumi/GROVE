### Question

How should I derive test cases for a component whose behavior depends on its current state and on the events it receives?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
When a component's behavior depends on its current state and the events it receives, SWEBOK v4 recommends **State Transition Testing**, a technique that models the System Under Test (SUT) as a **Finite-State Machine (FSM)** and derives test cases from that model. This falls under the broader category of **specification-based (black-box)** and **model-based** testing. Test suites should cover both typical and alternate event sequences, and automation harnesses are advised given the complexity of the technique.
