### Question

We are planning a project that depends on a third-party API nobody on the team has used and on a performance target we may not be able to reach. How should we handle these things that could go wrong, both when planning the work and while it is under way?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
SWEBOK v4 treats your two risks — an unfamiliar third-party API and an uncertain performance target — as textbook cases of uncertainty that must be converted into managed risks. During planning you should identify and prioritize them in a risk register, prototype the least-understood components first, express quality targets (including performance) in measurable terms, agree on abandonment conditions with stakeholders, and prefer an iterative or incremental life cycle so estimates can be revised as learning increases. During execution, risk reviews must continue at every iteration, the process must be actively monitored for whether objectives are on track, and automated testing should be used to surface integration and performance regressions as early as possible.
