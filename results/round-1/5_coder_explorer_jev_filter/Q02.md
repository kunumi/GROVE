- Apply a table-driven approach only in appropriate circumstances, because when used appropriately, [SWEBOK excerpt fdc469d7926f431a528f]. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Runtime Configuration and Internationalization in Software Design.md) [JEV 0.93]
- Decide [SWEBOK excerpt bc8a57df1f83a2f677b5]): identify the conditions and their associated results that currently live in the conditional chain, and represent them as table entries rather than as branching logic. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Runtime Configuration and Internationalization in Software Design.md) [JEV 0.85]
- Decide [SWEBOK excerpt ffd30c20bbf41330ba5d]: choose a lookup strategy (e.g., direct indexing, indexed lookup, or stair-step access) that matches the structure of the data and the performance requirements of the function. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Runtime Configuration and Internationalization in Software Design.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Treat the table-driven approach as a 'construction technique' under the broader goal of minimizing complexity; [SWEBOK excerpt 2a925e82b0ef7eebb1cf], using modular design and other specific coding techniques.
