A feature request I have to implement is written as loose prose, and the product owner and I keep reading it differently. How should the team pin down, together with the domain experts and before any code is written, what the feature must do, so that it cannot be misread?

# Guidelines
- Hold facilitated workshops (such as Joint Application Development / Joint Requirements Planning sessions) with both domain experts and the development team before writing any code, so that all parties negotiate and agree on what the feature must do in a shared session rather than through separate readings of a prose description. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Stakeholder Identification and Requirements Elicitation Techniques/Stakeholder Elicitation Techniques and Requirements Sources.md) [JEV 0.84]
- Make each requirement unambiguous (interpretable in only one way), testable/quantified (so compliance can be clearly demonstrated), and atomic (representing a single decision). These properties are the analysis checklist that reveals whether the current prose can be misread. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Analysis and Refinement/Software Requirements Properties and Quality Criteria.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Apply the 5-Whys technique: repeatedly ask 'Why is this the requirement?' until the true underlying problem is reached. This avoids specifying a proposed solution (which is what a loose prose feature request often describes) instead of the actual need, and typically converges in two or three cycles. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Analysis and Refinement/Software Requirements Properties and Quality Criteria.md) [JEV 0.97]
- Use structured natural language specification — for example the actor-action format, or a standard use-case template — to impose constraints on how requirements are expressed, increasing precision and conciseness compared with unstructured prose. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Specification and Validation Techniques/Natural Language Requirements Specifications/Structured Natural Language Requirements Specification.md) [JEV 0.97]
- Replace or supplement natural language with modeling languages (e.g., UML or SysML class/behavioral diagrams) to avoid the inherent ambiguity of prose. Structural models capture policies to enforce; behavioral models capture the dynamic behaviour the feature must exhibit. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Model-Based Requirements Specification Approaches.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Adopt Acceptance Test-Driven Development (ATDD) or Behaviour-Driven Development (BDD): before any design or construction begins, [SWEBOK excerpt c4f846d0ec328aa558ee] optionally QA/test professionals meet to agree on a concrete [SWEBOK excerpt 086c9116dac4bad840d6] prove the feature is correctly implemented. These tests become the unambiguous, executable definition of the feature. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Specification and Validation Techniques/Test-Driven Development and Acceptance Testing/Acceptance Criteria-Based Requirements Specification.md) [JEV 0.97]
- Conduct multi-perspective requirements reviews: have clients/users verify their needs are completely and accurately represented; have requirements-specification specialists check clarity and conformance to standards; and have design/construction engineers check that the specification is sufficient to support their work. Provide checklists or a 'definition of done' to focus reviewers. (source: root/Requirements Validation and Management/Requirements Validation Methods and Techniques.md) [JEV 0.98]
- Build a prototype (even a low-fidelity UI prototype) to make the team's interpretation of the feature concrete and visible to domain experts. Prototypes expose engineers' assumptions and give stakeholders the feedback needed to identify where those assumptions are wrong — including dynamic behaviour that is hard to convey in text. (source: root/Requirements Validation and Management/Prototyping for Requirements Validation and Feedback.md; root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Stakeholder Identification and Requirements Elicitation Techniques/Stakeholder Elicitation Techniques and Requirements Sources.md) [JEV 0.96]
- Verify that the full set of requirements is complete (addresses boundary conditions, exception conditions, and security needs), internally consistent (no requirement conflicts with another), and externally consistent (no requirement conflicts with source material). Gaps in completeness and consistency are primary reasons prose is read differently by different people. (source: root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Analysis and Refinement/Software Requirements Properties and Quality Criteria.md) [JEV 0.95]
- Treat requirements development as a whole [SWEBOK excerpt 54783f0fcae46877327a].' Continue the elicitation–analysis–specification–validation cycle iteratively — elicited information rarely arrives in final form and [SWEBOK excerpt 14030ce90c41ab9b65aa]. (source: root/Requirements Validation and Management/Prototyping for Requirements Validation and Feedback.md; root/Software Requirements_ Foundations and Engineering Practices/Requirements Engineering_ Elicitation, Analysis, and Specification/Requirements Analysis and Refinement/Requirements Analysis and Elicitation Process.md) [JEV 0.94]
