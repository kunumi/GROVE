It is distinguished by being driven by external environmental change, not by user requests for new features or existing defects. (source: root/Software Testing, Operations, and Maintenance/Software Operations and Maintenance Practices/Software Maintenance Roles and Cost Distribution/Software Maintenance Activities and Cost Distribution.md) [JEV 0.78]
