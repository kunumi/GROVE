- Apply defensive programming: protect routines from being broken by invalid inputs by checking the values of all [SWEBOK excerpt dda5a39412b3af36208d] before they propagate and cause failures. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Contract Design and Error Handling Strategies/Design by Contract and Defensive Programming.md) [JEV 0.98]
- Use assertions (executable predicates placed in a program) to perform runtime checks. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Parameterization, Templates, Generics, and Assertions.md; root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Assertions and Design by Contract in Programming.md) [JEV 0.96]
- Apply Design by Contract: specify preconditions and postconditions for each routine so that each routine or class forms [SWEBOK excerpt 13c5545a4a774df8cb56], clearly defining correct behavior and enabling reliable detection of contract violations at runtime. (source: root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Assertions and Design by Contract in Programming.md; root/Software Construction Techniques and Practices/Contract Design and Error Handling Strategies/Design by Contract and Defensive Programming.md) [JEV 0.95]
