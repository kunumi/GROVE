### Question

What kinds of changes are made to software that is already in use, and what distinguishes each kind from the others?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

Here are the kinds of changes made to software already in use, as described in the SWEBOK Guide:

1. **Corrective maintenance** addresses defects and faults found in the software. It is distinguished by its reactive nature — it responds to failures or incorrect behavior discovered during operation, fixing bugs so the software performs as originally intended. (source: "corrective maintenance: reactive modification of a software product performed after delivery to correct discovered problems")

2. **Adaptive maintenance** modifies the software to keep it usable in a changed or changing environment. It is distinguished by its focus on external factors — changes in hardware, operating systems, regulations, or other environmental conditions force updates to the software even though the software itself was not broken. (source: "adaptive maintenance: modification of a software product performed after delivery to keep a software product usable in a changed or changing environment")

3. **Perfective maintenance** improves performance or maintainability, or adds new capabilities requested by users. It is distinguished by its proactive and enhancement-oriented nature — the software works correctly, but users or stakeholders want it to do more or do it better. (source: "[SWEBOK excerpt 4102d6b8711250be31a9] improve performance or maintainability")

4. **Preventive maintenance** is performed to detect and correct latent faults before they become actual faults or failures. It is distinguished by its anticipatory nature — problems have not yet manifested, but the software is modified to reduce the risk of future failures. (source: "preventive [SWEBOK excerpt 84b49ac2ec46d0a41b55] detect and correct latent faults in the software product before they become effective faults")
