### Question

At the end of each release our bug tracker tells us only how many bugs were found, and that count gives the team nothing to act on. What should we do with our bug records so that they help us introduce fewer bugs in later releases?

### Answer

# Summary

Bug counts alone are insufficient for process improvement. Teams should classify, record, and analyze defect data to identify root causes, understand trends, build defect profiles, and drive corrective and preventive actions that reduce recurrence in future releases.

# Guidelines

- Classify bugs by type rather than counting them alone, since numbers without classification are insufficient to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence. (source: "[SWEBOK excerpt cc445036fb1c8bc4d733]." — Corrective and Preventive Actions in Quality Management.md)
