# Summary

Before implementing a proposed multi-service split, the team should evaluate the high-level architecture against competing quality goals using structured methods, scenario-based analysis, explicit tradeoff reasoning, and multiple-criteria decision techniques—before writing any code.

- Use the Architecture Tradeoff Analysis Method (ATAM) to systematically evaluate the proposed structure against competing quality attributes such as response time, cost, and modifiability; ATAM uses a utility tree and scenarios to surface tradeoffs among quality requirements.
