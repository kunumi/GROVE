### Answer

# Summary

A routine can make its assumptions explicit and guard against invalid inputs by using Design by Contract (preconditions/postconditions), defensive programming, and assertions. These techniques clarify what a routine expects and produces, detect mismatched interface assumptions, and define how bad inputs are handled—improving correctness, robustness, and overall software quality.
