### Answer

# Summary

Good module organization requires applying principles like high cohesion, loose coupling, encapsulation, and separation of concerns to keep software understandable and maintainable. Existing poorly structured programs can be improved through refactoring—reorganizing code without changing external behavior—and reverse engineering to understand current structure before restructuring.
