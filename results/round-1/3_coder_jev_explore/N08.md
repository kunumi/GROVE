### Answer

# Summary

Document recurring workflows so both humans and automated agents can follow them consistently. Write-ups must specify required inputs, transforming activities, outputs, controls, and enabling mechanisms. Multiple notation forms are acceptable, from natural language to formal diagrams. Clear roles, responsibilities, and rationale for decisions must be included.
