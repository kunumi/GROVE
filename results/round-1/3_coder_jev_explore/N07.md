### Answer

# Summary

When a project depends on an unfamiliar third-party API or a potentially unreachable performance target, teams should identify these as risks early, analyze their probability and impact, build mitigation strategies, prototype or simulate to validate feasibility, monitor risks continuously throughout the project, and document all decisions so the team can adapt when reality diverges from the plan.
