### Question

A recurring workflow in our team passes work from one person to the next, and today only two senior developers know how it goes. I want to write it down so that new developers and automated coding agents follow it the same way every time: what must the write-up state, and in what forms can it be written?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

Here are guidelines for writing up your recurring workflow, with sources from the SWEBOK:

**1. State the purpose, inputs, and outputs of each step in the workflow.**
Processes must have required inputs, transforming activities, and outputs generated clearly described so that each step is unambiguous.
