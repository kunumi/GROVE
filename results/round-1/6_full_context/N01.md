### Question

A feature request I have to implement is written as loose prose, and the product owner and I keep reading it differently. How should the team pin down, together with the domain experts and before any code is written, what the feature must do, so that it cannot be misread?

### Answer

1. **Identify all stakeholder classes and involve them explicitly.** Different stakeholders (clients, customers, users, SMEs, operations staff) have distinct perspectives. Perform a stakeholder analysis to reduce the risk of requirements being biased toward better-represented groups and away from less well-represented ones.
