# Guidelines
- Identify all risk factors early: treat the unfamiliar third-party API and the uncertain performance target as explicit risks. For each one, record it in a risk register that captures probability, potential impact, and planned responses. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Risk Management_ Identification, Analysis, and Mitigation Strategies.md) [JEV 0.92]
- Analyze and prioritize risks by assessing the [SWEBOK excerpt cdbc51435e3683410db0] (e.g., the API being unusable, the performance target being unachievable), then rank them so the most dangerous uncertainties receive the most attention. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Risk Management_ Identification, Analysis, and Mitigation Strategies.md) [JEV 0.97]
- Develop risk mitigation strategies for each identified risk — strategies intended to reduce the probability that a negative event occurs and to minimize its impact if it does occur. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Risk Management_ Identification, Analysis, and Mitigation Strategies.md) [JEV 0.98]
- Use recognized risk assessment methods — such as expert judgment, historical data, and decision trees or process simulations — to evaluate the two risk factors. Involve all relevant stakeholders and, if necessary, establish project abandonment conditions up front. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Risk Assessment Methods and Software Project Risk Management.md) [JEV 0.95]
- Pay particular attention to software quality requirements (such as the performance target) when managing risk, since these have unique characteristics that affect the entire risk management approach. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Risk Assessment Methods and Software Project Risk Management.md) [JEV 0.81]
- Do not treat risk management as a one-time planning activity. Revisit and update the risk register [SWEBOK excerpt 13152c3c13d0875a6efb] so that emerging problems with the API integration or performance are caught and addressed early. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Continuous Risk and Quality Management/Risk Management Throughout Project Life Cycle.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Define software quality requirements (including the performance target) both quantitatively and qualitatively at the start of the project, so that the team has a clear, measurable criterion against which to track progress and detect shortfalls. (source: root/Risk Management and Quality Assessment/Continuous Risk and Quality Management/Quality Management in Software Projects.md) [JEV 0.88]
- Consider adopting an iterative or incremental life cycle so that time and cost estimates can be routinely updated as the team's understanding of the third-party API and performance constraints grows through successive development cycles. (source: root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Engineering Processes, Lifecycles, and Measurement/Predictive, Iterative, Evolutionary, and Incremental Life Cycles.md) [JEV 0.94]
- Embrace an Agile mindset — which values openness to change and commitment to technical excellence — rather than locking in a purely predictive plan that assumes the API behaviour and performance attainability are fully known from the start. (source: root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Engineering Processes, Lifecycles, and Measurement/Software Development Models and Iterative Approaches/Agile Principles and Methodology Fundamentals/Agile Software Development Values and Practices.md) [JEV 0.89]
