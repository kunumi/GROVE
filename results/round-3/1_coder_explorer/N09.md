### Question

I must choose one of three candidate libraries for our product. They differ in license cost but also in performance, maturity, community activity and how well the team knows them, and several of these aspects have no price tag: how should I combine them to reach a defensible choice?

### Answer

# Summary
Choosing among candidate libraries that differ in both cost and unquantifiable attributes (performance, maturity, community, team familiarity) is classified by SWEBOK v4 as a **multiple-attribute decision**. The BoK prescribes (1) naming all criteria explicitly and expressing each as objectively as possible, (2) treating non-monetary factors as "intangible assets" to be characterised by quality and impact, (3) normalising those indicators, and then (4) collapsing them into a defensible choice through either compensatory techniques (additive weighting, AHP) or non-compensatory techniques (dominance, satisficing). Risk management of each candidate's uncertainty dimensions and consideration of a "do-nothing" fallback complete the framework.
