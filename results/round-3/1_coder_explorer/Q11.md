### Question

What kinds of changes are made to software that is already in use, and what distinguishes each kind from the others?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary
SWEBOK v4 identifies several distinct categories of changes made to software already in use, each distinguished by the **intent or reason** behind the change: corrective (fixing faults), adaptive (responding to environmental changes), perfective (improving quality or adding enhancements), additive (larger-scale new functionality), emergency (temporary unscheduled fixes pending proper correction), preventive (proactively guarding against threats or obsolescence), and retirement (managed end-of-life). A common misconception is that most maintenance is corrective; studies cited in the BoK show that over 80% is actually used for enhancing and adapting software.
