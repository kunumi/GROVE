### Question

What kinds of changes are made to software that is already in use, and what distinguishes each kind from the others?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

Here are the kinds of changes made to software already in use, with distinguishing characteristics drawn from the SWEBOK Guide:

1. **Corrective maintenance** addresses faults (defects or bugs) discovered in the software after delivery. The change is reactive: it is triggered by a failure or malfunction that the software exhibits during operation, and the goal is to fix something that is broken. (source: "Corrective maintenance is reactive modification of a software product performed after delivery to correct discovered problems")

2. **Adaptive maintenance** modifies the software to keep it usable in a changed or changing environment. The environment may include the operating system, hardware platform, or other software with which the product interacts; the software itself may not be broken, but it must be updated to remain compatible. (source: "Adaptive maintenance is modification of a software product performed after delivery to keep a software product usable in a changed or changing environment")

3. **Perfective maintenance** improves performance or maintainability, or adds new capabilities requested by users. Unlike corrective maintenance, nothing is wrong; the motivation is enhancement rather than repair. (source: "Perfective maintenance is [SWEBOK excerpt c137e304c4117aa9bd25] improve performance or maintainability")

4. **Preventive maintenance** modifies the software to detect and correct latent faults before they manifest as actual failures. It is distinguished from corrective maintenance in that the fault has not yet caused a failure; the intent is to reduce the likelihood of future problems. (source: "Preventive maintenance is [SWEBOK excerpt c137e304c4117aa9bd25] detect and correct latent faults in the software product before they become effective faults")
