### Question

The same kind of failure keeps coming back in production even though every occurrence was patched as soon as it was reported. What methods can the team use to trace the failure back to what is really producing it, so that the fix keeps it from happening again?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary

Recurring production failures require teams to go beyond surface-level patching and investigate underlying causes. Key methods include root cause analysis, defect classification, measurement and trend analysis, monitoring and logging, traceability, problem resolution processes, and corrective/preventive action systems to prevent recurrence.

# Guidelines

- Classify defects by type and category rather than tracking numbers alone, since numbers without classification are insufficient to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence.
