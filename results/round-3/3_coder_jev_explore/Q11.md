What kinds of changes are made to software that is already in use, and what distinguishes each kind from the others?

### Answer

# Summary

Software maintenance involves several distinct types of changes to operational software: corrective, adaptive, perfective, additive, preventive, and emergency maintenance. Each type addresses a different purpose—fixing faults, responding to environmental shifts, enhancing quality, adding features, preventing future problems, or providing urgent temporary fixes.

# Guidelines

- **Corrective maintenance** addresses faults and latent defects in operational software.
