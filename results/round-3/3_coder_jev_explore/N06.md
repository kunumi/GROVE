### Question

At the end of each release our bug tracker tells us only how many bugs were found, and that count gives the team nothing to act on. What should we do with our bug records so that they help us introduce fewer bugs in later releases?

Answer with a list of guidelines. After each guideline, cite the source it comes from: the path of the SWEBOK file if you have it, otherwise a verbatim quote of at least 8 words from the passage, in the form (source: ...).

### Answer

# Summary

Bug counts alone are insufficient to prevent recurrence. Teams should classify defects into meaningful taxonomies, record and store defect data, perform root cause analysis, apply statistical and trend analysis, develop defect profiles, and implement corrective and preventive actions to continuously improve software engineering processes and reduce future bugs.

# Guidelines

- Classify defects into a meaningful taxonomy rather than counting numbers alone, since numbers without classification are insufficient to identify underlying causes and prevent recurrence.
