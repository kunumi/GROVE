### Answer

# Summary

Before implementing a proposed split of a large module into networked services, the team should evaluate the high-level architecture against competing quality goals using structured methods, scenario-based analysis, explicit tradeoff reasoning, documented decision rationale, and multiple architecture views—without writing any code.
