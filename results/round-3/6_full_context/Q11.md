### Question

What kinds of changes are made to software that is already in use, and what distinguishes each kind from the others?

### Answer

Here is a list of guidelines describing the kinds of changes made to software already in use, with what distinguishes each:

---

1. **Corrective maintenance** addresses faults and latent defects discovered after delivery. It is reactive — triggered by observed failures or defects in the operational software.
(source: Software Testing, Operations, and Maintenance/Software Operations and Maintenance Practices/Software Maintenance Roles and Cost Distribution/Software Maintenance Activities and Cost Distribution.md)

2. **Preventive maintenance** acts before failures occur, making changes to prevent future problems such as security threats or technical obsolescence. It is proactive rather than reactive.
(source: Software Testing, Operations, and Maintenance/Software Operations and Maintenance Practices/Software Maintenance Roles and Cost Distribution/Software Maintenance Activities and Cost Distribution.md)

3. **Adaptive maintenance** modifies software to accommodate changes in the interfaced systems or infrastructure — that is, the software itself has not failed, but its environment has changed and the software must be updated to remain compatible.
(source: Software Testing, Operations, and Maintenance/Software Operations and Maintenance Practices/Software Maintenance Roles and Cost Distribution/Software Maintenance Activities and Cost Distribution.md)

4. **Additive maintenance** [SWEBOK excerpt 94bdfb398254122be640]. It is distinguished from perfective maintenance in that it adds functionality with relatively large additions or changes.
(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt f5ac630d0543a2ba3a3b].")

5. **Perfective maintenance** improves the software for users through enhancements, improved documentation, and recoding to improve performance or other software attributes — without necessarily adding entirely new capabilities. It differs from additive maintenance in the scale and nature of change.
(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt e5fe30859351837b8f04].")

6. **Emergency maintenance** is [SWEBOK excerpt 29afddbf422c4d134083]. It is distinguished from corrective maintenance by being temporary and unplanned rather than a full, planned fix.
(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt 3dca8108d3ba2c28d925].")
