### Part 1 — Organizing Modules for Maintainability

**1. Keep modules small and name them clearly.**

**2. Give every module a single, clearly bounded responsibility.**
Following Parnas's principle, each module in a system should carry one responsibility. Modularization is a special case of *separation of concerns* and *divide and conquer*: distinct functionalities and responsibilities belong in distinct components.
