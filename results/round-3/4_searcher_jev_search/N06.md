## Guidelines for Turning Bug Records into Defect-Prevention Tools

---

### 1. Stop recording raw counts alone — also capture type and classification

Tracking only numbers is insufficient. *"[SWEBOK excerpt cc445036fb1c8bc4d733]."* Extend each record with fields that describe what kind of defect it is.

(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt cc445036fb1c8bc4d733]." — `/workspace/swebok-v4/root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Quality Assurance and Verification Standards/Software Quality Assurance and Defect Management/Quality Metrics, Reliability Models, and Corrective Actions/Corrective and Preventive Actions in Quality Management.md`)

---

### 2. Establish and apply a meaningful defect classification taxonomy

Each defect record should be tagged according to a consistent taxonomy. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 20435f5e63e97ce04745]."* Taxonomies can be generic or specific to a quality attribute (e.g., security vulnerabilities, usability defects).

(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt bcc82cdbad3e6dcf9632]" — `/workspace/swebok-v4/root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Quality Assurance and Verification Standards/Software Quality Assurance and Defect Management/Software Quality Management and Defect Classification.md`)

---

### 3. Record all defect data — data not recorded is data lost

Defect records must be persisted in a system, not just resolved and closed. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 1bd4e9984ae7478574ad]."*

(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt 1bd4e9984ae7478574ad]." — `/workspace/swebok-v4/root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Quality Assurance and Verification Standards/Software Quality Assurance and Defect Management/Software Quality Management and Defect Classification.md`)

---

### 4. Perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA) on the recorded defects

Go beyond fixing the symptom: analyse why each defect was introduced. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 866261af5d5a2dcf15a3]."* Use techniques such as:
- **5-whys** — [SWEBOK excerpt dc9c291b07ca19050f46] (Ishikawa/fishbone) diagrams** — break down contributing causes in successive levels of detail.
- **Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)** — formal and/or cause-effect chains.
- **Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA)** — forward-chain from elements that can fail to their effects.

(source: "[SWEBOK excerpt 866261af5d5a2dcf15a3]." — `/workspace/swebok-v4/root/Software Engineering Processes and Quality Assurance/Software Quality Assurance and Verification Standards/Software Quality Assurance and Defect Management/Software Quality Management and Defect Classification.md`)

(source: "The 5-whys technique…[SWEBOK excerpt e524a6814dc318ae160a]." — `/workspace/swebok-v4/root/Engineering Decision-Making Through Data and Standards/Standards, Quality Assurance, and Modern Manufacturing Systems/Root Cause Analysis Methods and Application/Root Cause Analysis Techniques.md`)

### 5. Prioritize which defects to investigate using Pareto analysis and frequency-severity scoring

Not all defects deserve equal analytical effort. *"[SWEBOK excerpt b8619cf04eec5a75c3e3]."*

### 6. Apply statistical and graphical analysis to understand defect trends and types

Use visualisation tools on the accumulated defect data. *"Software quality measurement also [SWEBOK excerpt ddd77427515d2f2ed0b5]."* Useful techniques include descriptive statistics (run charts, scatter plots), trend analysis (control charts), and predictive reliability models.

### 7. Build defect profiles for the domain and use them to focus quality effort in the next release

Accumulated classified data should be turned into a reusable profile. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 0b10e71f8cf919ff803e]."*

### 8. Use past fault history to guide test design for the next release (error guessing)

### 9. Translate root causes into documented corrective and preventive actions, then monitor them

Identifying a root cause without acting on it is worthless. *"[SWEBOK excerpt dbe8929acdba854e2dfa]… [SWEBOK excerpt 3b338484aea1dce0cd50]."* These actions must be reported to an independent body that monitors their progress and informs stakeholders.

### 10. Feed defect analysis results into a retrospective and organisational learning database

At the end of a release, analyse the data formally and store lessons learned. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 238bfc70468a6a5f0b12]. [SWEBOK excerpt 8d1b90f05e370d9ee05c]."*

### 11. Ensure every measurement of defects is tied to a specific decision — avoid "metrics for the curious"

Before adding any new defect metric to your tracker, check that it supports an actual decision. *"[SWEBOK excerpt 73437b3f379b441d33c5]."*
